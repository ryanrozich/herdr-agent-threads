What changed

  • Each side keeps a margin of 8% of the pane, never under 24 px or over 56 px. The text takes the rest up to the reading line, and a wider pane splits the spare room evenly. Full uses the same margins.
  • Bubbles and replies end at the same right edge. The 52 px a bubble ran past the reply is gone.
  • The kept width is the reading line plus 56 px each side: 573 px at text size 14 (it was 545), 704 at Wide (it was 676). Cmd-Shift-T and Keep the pane at this width use it.
  • Rail ticks are 10 px wide, 16 when selected, 14 px in from the pane's edge, and the whole margin up to 32 px takes the click. In a margin under 40 px they are 8 and 12 px, 6 px in.
  • Reading lines are 1.57 times the text size, 22 px at 14, and the height estimates use the same number.
  • No notice, hint or new setting.
