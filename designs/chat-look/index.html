Agent Threads, drawn as a chat

A design proposal for the owner's request: make the panel look and feel like the chat apps people already use, keep every piece of information, and collapse long prompts and replies. Pin access and quote tracking are folded in. Nothing here is built. All content is made up.

Recommendation

  • Your messages become bubbles on the right, and replies become plain text across the pane. These are the conventions chat apps share. There is no other app's branding, colour or layout, and Herdr's own colours, type and icons stay.
  • Every turn shows your message and the start of its reply, without opening it. Today a row shows one or the other until you open the card. Long text collapses with "Show more": your message at 4 lines and the reply at 6.
  • The numbers stay. Turn number and time sit above your message. Duration, tokens, model and tool calls sit under the reply, with the copy, quote and pin buttons.
  • Pins move into a Pinned view. It opens as a second pane beside Threads when there is room, and as an All and Pinned switch in the same pane when there is not. It replaces the tray.
  • Quote tracking marks the parts of a reply you quoted later and links both ways. It ships after the chat look, as its own change.
  • Order: pinning (PR 6) first as it is, then the chat look with the Pinned view, then quote tracking.

Your decisions

1. Is the chat look the direction?

Bubbles for your messages, plain replies, numbers above and below. Recommended: yes. Every state is compared with today below.

2. How long before text collapses?

Your message at 4 lines and the reply at 6 (today they are 2 and 3). Recommended: 4 and 6. The settings panel keeps both adjustable. The reason is in Collapse lengths.

3. How do you reach pins?

A: an All and Pinned switch at the top of the pane. B: a Pinned pane beside Threads. C: keep the tray, improved. Recommended: B, falling back to A when the window is too narrow. They are one Pinned view drawn in two places, so this costs about the same as A alone.

4. Quote tracking: now or after?

Recommended: after the chat look, as its own pull request. It is drawn on the chat look's reply text, so building it on the current cards would mean drawing it twice.

Collapse lengths

I counted lines in the owner's own sessions from the last 10 days (60 files, about 4,700 typed messages and 4,800 final replies), from line lengths only, at the default pane width of 520 px:

Shown whole when collapsed at2 lines346812
Your messages19%27%31%40%
Replies36%46%53%58%66%

Six reply lines show about half of replies whole. Eight add only 5 points but cost two lines on every turn, so fewer turns fit on screen. Four lines of your message keep the bubble short and show the first lines of a longer request, which is usually the ask. At 280 px both figures drop (21% of messages at 4 lines, 41% of replies at 6), so the narrow pane collapses more often, and "Show more" stays one click or Enter away. A collapsed reply keeps its formatting (headings, bullets, code), cut at a line, never mid-word.

Reaching pins

The owner's words: "I can pin but not sure how to open the pin back up? clicking on it doesn't seem to open it.... oh maybe it does but it also stays pinned at the bottom.. I wonder about this interface." Each option below runs the same story: three pins (#8 left at its last heading, #7 not read yet, #11 still working); open #8, go on to #7, pin #10 from the feed, unpin #11, and a narrow pane.

In every option, opening a pin lands on the place you left it, with one line above saying the turn's top is further up, and the pin you are in says Reading. Pins keep "New reply", the working spinner and the place they keep today, and a pinned reply says how much of it you have quoted (see Quote tracking).

A. An All and Pinned switch

The pane shows one list at a time. v or a click switches. In Pinned, an opened pin unfolds in place under its card. The switch shows the pin count, with a dot when a pinned turn has a new reply. Simple, and it works at any width, but you cannot watch the feed while working through pins.

B. A Pinned pane beside Threads (recommended)

The Threads pane stays as it is. v or the header button opens a second pane of the same mod to its right, listing the pins. You work through pins on the right and still see new turns arrive on the left. Pinning from the feed adds the pin on the right straight away, and both panes share one pins.json, which PR 6 already supports.

Can a mod do this? Yes, with a command it already uses. Threads opens itself with pane.split (direction right, its own mod, its own props) when you press Cmd-Shift-T, and the same command can split the Threads pane with a view: "pinned" prop. layout.resize sets the widths. Two Threads panes on one session already share pins through pins.json. What has not been tried is calling pane.split from a key press inside the pane rather than from the open command. A short spike proves that before the build.

A narrow window. When the Threads pane is under about 600 px, splitting would leave both panes under 300 px. So Pinned opens as the switch from option A in the same pane, and a toast says why. Closing the Pinned pane is closing any pane, and nothing is lost.

C. The tray, improved

Today's tray stays above the footer. The pin you opened says Reading and has an accent bar, the turn in the list carries a pin, and the toast says where you landed. It is the smallest change, but the tray still takes rows from the list, and with five pins it folds to chips in a short pane.

Quote tracking

The owner's words: "I also wonder whether it's possible to indicate that a portion of a reply was quoted and link to the quoted message fragment we used later.. to also help us keep track of what parts of which replies we have already replied to as we work through various messages and updates".

How it looks

  • In the reply, a quoted part gets a tinted background, an accent bar and a link under it: "You replied in #9" (narrow: "#9"). The reply's note line reads "Quoted in #9 and #10 · 2 of 3 paragraphs answered". It is the same line and style as today's "Answered in #N", so both read as one idea: where this turn was picked up.
  • In the later message, the quote shows as today, with a bar, and its first line is a link: "From #8".
  • Following a link (click, or l) scrolls to the other end and flashes it briefly. Esc goes back.

How a quote is found

By matching, not by recording q. When the panel reads a session, each prompt's > lines are compared with the earlier replies. Recording q would miss quotes typed by hand, pasted from elsewhere, or made before the feature existed, and would need a file. Matching works the same for all five agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi and Cursor), because it reads only the prompt text each transcript already holds.

  • Edited quotes. Text is compared after folding case, spaces and Markdown marks, sentence by sentence. A quote you trimmed or changed still marks the sentences that match. A quote with no sentence in common marks nothing.
  • The same text twice. The nearest earlier reply wins, and within it the first match not already claimed by another quote.
  • Pasted wrappers. Tags an agent writes around pasted text in its transcript are stripped before comparing.
  • On disk. Nothing is stored. The marks come from the transcript each time, like "Answered in #N".
  • With pins. A pinned reply says "2 of 3 paragraphs answered, in #9 and #10", or "Not quoted yet". That is how you see what is left in a pinned reply.

How this was made

  • agent-threads-chat-look.pen is the design source, 63 frames. gen-pen.mjs writes it (bun designs/chat-look/gen-pen.mjs), with the running panel's own colours in dark and light.
  • make-session.mjs writes the made-up session. Today's screenshots are that session in the real panel, on Herdr 0.1.13 in an isolated home, at 520 and 280 px.
  • This page was checked in a recorded cloud browser (Cloudflare Browser Run): it loads, every comparison image loads, an image opens full size, the arrow keys step and Esc closes, and at 390 px there is no sideways scroll. The step screenshots and a GIF are in evidence/.
  • Widths: wide is the default 520 px, narrow is 280 px. A frame is a pane, not the whole window, except option B, which shows both panes.
