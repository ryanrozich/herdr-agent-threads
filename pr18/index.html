Day headers in the Pinned column

Pinned rows show only a time, so a pill now says which day each pin is from. Shots are of the synthetic acme-web session, taken on 11 Nov, so Today is Wed 11 Nov. Before is main at fd928a8; after is this branch. Click any shot to open it full size; arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What to test

  1. Install. Reinstall Agent Threads from the pin-days branch.
  2. Pins across days. In a session that spans more than one day, pin a turn from an earlier day and one from today with p. The Pinned column shows a small pill (Yesterday, Today, or the weekday and date) above the first pin, and another above each pin whose day differs from the pin above it.
  3. Pin order is kept. Pin one more turn from the earlier day. It goes to the bottom, as before, under its own pill, so that day's pill shows twice. Nothing regroups or re-sorts.
  4. One day. In a session from one day, every pin sits under a single pill at the top.
  5. Digit keys. In the column, press 1 to 5, or use Up and Down. The selection moves between pins only, never onto a pill, and a pin brought to the top keeps its pill above it.
  6. Narrow view. Drag Threads under 480 px and press v. The Pinned view in the same pane shows the same pills.
  7. Midnight. Leave Threads open past midnight. Today's pills turn into Yesterday on their own within a second (the list's day bands do too).
