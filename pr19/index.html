Pinned column in time order

Pins now run oldest first, so each day shows once, and their numbers follow that order. Shots are of the synthetic acme-web session, taken on 11 Nov, so Today is Wed 11 Nov. Before is main at a67b7dd; after is this branch. Click any shot to open it full size; arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What to test

  1. Install. Reinstall Agent Threads from the pin-time-order branch. Your saved pins stay; nothing needs moving.
  2. Your session. Open the Pinned column on the session where Monday showed twice. The pins now run oldest first, so each day shows once under its pill, and a lower turn number never sits under a higher one.
  3. Numbers follow the order. The top pin is 1. Press 1 to 4: each goes to the pin at that place. The list on the left shows the same number beside each pinned turn.
  4. Pin an older turn. Pin a turn older than your pins with p. It goes to the top as 1, and the others move down a number. The toast says which digit brings you back.
  5. Unpin. Unpin one with p. The ones below it move up a number. u brings it back to its place.
  6. Narrow view. Drag Threads under 480 px and press v: the same order and numbers.
