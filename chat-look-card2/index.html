The Turn card opens beside its info icon

The owner's two changes to PR 47: the info icon shows on a Worked for line whether it is open or closed, and the card opens beside the icon instead of at the bottom of the pane. Real frames from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini, on a synthetic session. Both builds get the same first presses on turn #7: select it, open it (its line stays closed), press i. Click any frame to open it full size. The arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What changed since the first version

  • The info icon ends every finished turn's Worked for line, open or closed: dim on the selected turn, accent while its card is open, and on other turns only while the pointer is on the line.
  • The card opens beside the icon, its right edge on the icon's right edge, downward, or upward when the line sits low in the pane.
  • i opens it beside the selected turn's line and scrolls that line on screen first. ↑ and ↓ move it to the next turn's line.
  • A click on the icon opens or closes the card and does not open or close the line.
  • While the card is open, any click closes it and does nothing else, and so do i and Esc. A running turn has no card until it finishes. The card's content is unchanged.

Two limits, both from how Herdr draws a list

  • A list row cannot carry its own click handlers, so the card cannot either. That is why any click closes it, including a click on the card or its close mark.
  • Herdr reports a click on a closed turn's row by the row alone, with no position up or down. At the icon's distance from the left edge the click could as well be on the message bubble above it. So on a closed turn a click on the icon opens the turn, as a click anywhere on that row does, and from there the icon opens the card. i opens it on any finished turn. In an open turn the line is a row of its own, so the icon there takes the click.

Left for the owner to check by hand

  • Point at the Worked for line of a turn that is not selected: the icon should show at its right end.
  • In an open turn, click the icon: the card should open beside it, and the line should not change.
  • With the card open, press ↑ and ↓: it should move to each turn's line. Click anywhere, i or Esc: it should close.
