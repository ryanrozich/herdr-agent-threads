Agent Threads · PR 27

The image viewer with the prompt and reply beside it

Each pair puts the approved mock beside the real build. The real shots come from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini, running this branch on a synthetic acme-web session. That session's turn 5 has two pasted images where the mock has three, so the image counts and labels differ. Click any shot to open it full size; the arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What to try

  1. Install. cd ~/herdr-agent-threads-try && git fetch && git checkout main && git pull && ./install.sh
  2. Open the viewer. Open a turn with pasted screenshots, click a tile, then click the larger image. The viewer fills the window with your prompt and the reply in a 500 px panel on the right.
  3. Step the images. ← →, the round buttons or a thumbnail. The [Image #N] label of the image on screen is marked in your prompt, and a click on another label goes to that image. 1 to 9 jump.
  4. Read the reply. j k or Space put the next paragraph at the top; scroll with the trackpad, then j goes on from there.
  5. Hide and copy. i hides the panel, and the next viewer opens the same way. c copies the reply as Markdown.
  6. Narrow window. Under 1,100 px wide the panel docks under the image, about 300 px tall.

Wide window, dark, 1600 × 1000

The panel is 500 px on the right. The prompt sits in its bubble with the label of the image on screen marked, then the tool-call line, the reply as Markdown with its code box (language, Copy, colours), the stats and Copy reply. The thumbnails sit under the image.

Narrow window, light

The same dock in a light theme, on the phone screenshot: a tall image shrinks to fit the stage.

Panel hidden (i)

With the panel hidden the image takes the whole width, and the Reply button shows it again. The next viewer opens the same way.

j and k in real Herdr

A narrow light window. Each press puts the next part of the panel at its top: the caption, then each paragraph of the reply. k goes back one.

Where the build differs from the mock

  • A message with one image still shows its one thumbnail under the image.
  • Tables in the reply are Herdr's own Markdown tables, with a header fill and cell borders, as in the open turn.
  • For a turn still running, the panel shows the reply as it was when the viewer opened, and its head says so.
  • ↑ ↓ step the images as before. On Herdr before 0.1.15, or with the panel hidden, j k step the images too.
