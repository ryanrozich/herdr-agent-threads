Outline view: one row per turn

o folds the list into an outline, so finding an older turn is a few key presses instead of a long scroll. Shots are of the synthetic acme-web session in an isolated test window. Before is main at 3e2f836; after is the outline-view branch. Click any shot to open it full size; the arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What to test

  1. Install. cd ~/herdr-agent-threads-try && git fetch && git checkout outline-view && git pull && ./install.sh
  2. Open the outline. In a session with many turns, press o. Each turn is one row: its number, time, the first line of your prompt, and a pin with its number if it is pinned. A session over several days keeps its day bands, and the day strip shows while you scroll.
  3. Move. ↑ ↓ or j k move one row. [ ] go by day, Home and End to the first and newest turn.
  4. Open a turn. Press Enter or Space, or click a row. The outline closes and that turn is open in the list, selected, with its head at the top of the pane.
  5. Go back. Press o, move a few rows, then Esc (or o again). The list comes back as it was before, with the same turn selected and the same place on screen.
  6. Search, agents and pins. In the outline, / and a word leave only the matching turns, with the match marked; the first Esc clears the search, the next leaves the outline. a shows or hides agent messages. p pins the selected turn and its row gets a pin; 1 returns to that pin in the list.
  7. Narrow and keys. Make Threads narrow (under 300 px the time gives way). Press ?: the sheet lists o. o no longer opens a turn; Enter and Space do. A quoteKey of o is still refused.
