How pasted images work on the stack today

Real frames from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini, running the mod from the top of the stack (PR 42, width, which holds PRs 29 to 41). The session is synthetic and the images are drawn placeholders. The image flow has not changed; this is what is there now. Click any frame to open it full size. The arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

3 clicks

From a resting list to the full-screen review, with no keys. No key gets there: Enter or Space opens a turn, but nothing on the keyboard opens an image.

  1. Click the turn. It opens and the reply shows in full.
  2. Click a tile. That image shows large under the tiles.
  3. Click the large image. The viewer opens on it, with the prompt and reply beside it.

Notes

  • A turn that is still running is the exception: its tiles sit at the top of the turn, and one click on a tile opens the viewer.
  • Opening the work (step 4) does not change the count. The images and the work are separate.
  • The panel is 560 px wide and the list frames are cropped to it. The viewer frames are the whole test window.
  • No code changed for this page. A one-click path is being designed separately.
