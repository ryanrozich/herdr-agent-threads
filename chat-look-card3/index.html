The Turn card shows when the pointer rests on its icon

The owner asked to see a turn's numbers without clicking or opening the turn. Each finished turn's info icon now carries a hover card: rest the pointer on it and the card shows beside it, move off and it goes. Real frames from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini, on a synthetic session. That window cannot move a pointer, so no frame here shows the hover itself. What the frames do show: the build draws with a hover card in every row, with no error and no re-render banner, the icon sits in place on a closed turn, and i still opens the card. Click any frame to open it full size. The arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What changed

  • While no card is pinned, every finished turn's info icon carries a hover card. Rest the pointer on it for 0.3 s and the card shows beside it, on an open or a closed turn. Move off it, and off the card, and it goes after 0.2 s.
  • Nothing opens and the selection does not move, so you can point at one closed turn after another.
  • The hover copy of the card has no close mark, since moving off closes it. Its content is otherwise the same.
  • i, and a click on the icon in an open turn, still pin the card as before. While it is pinned there are no hover cards, so only one card shows.
  • Every row on screen now carries a card. On a 2,000-turn session a draw takes about 0.5 ms more, against Herdr's 50 ms limit.

Left for the owner to check by hand

  • With turns closed, rest the pointer on the info icon at the right end of a Worked for line. The card should show beside it, and the turn should stay closed.
  • Move to the next turn's icon. The first card should go and the next should show.
  • Move from the icon onto the card. It should stay while the pointer is on it.
