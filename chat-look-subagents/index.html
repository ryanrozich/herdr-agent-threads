Subagents get a line of their own: "Ran 3 subagents"

Step 1 of round 5. A turn's subagent calls no longer share a line with the commands around them. They get their own step group with the bot icon, and opened, one row per subagent with its task name. Nothing new is read: states, the report and what each one is doing come in later steps. Real frames from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini, on a synthetic session whose newest turn starts three subagents in the background between two commands. Before is main, after is this PR. Click any frame to open it full size. The arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

What this step does not do yet

  • The check on "Ran 3 subagents" means the calls were read and none failed. For a subagent started in the background, that is its launch, not its result. States from the task notifications ("3 working", "1 failed", a time per row) are step 2.
  • A press on a subagent row shows the launch message, not its report. The report is step 4.
  • Claude Code's task list tools (TaskCreate, TaskUpdate and the like) start with "Task" and used to be counted as handoffs. They now count as other tools.
