Long messages show in full, with no Show more

The owner found the blue Show more links distracting and asked for full replies by default, with shortening as a setting to opt into. Real frames from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini, on a synthetic session. Both builds start with turn #7 selected and closed, then ↑. Click any frame to open it full size. The arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

Frame by frame

What changed

  • A new setting, truncate, shown as Shorten long messages in the settings panel with a scissors icon. It is off by default.
  • Off: a closed turn shows the whole prompt and the whole reply as Markdown. An open turn shows the whole message, and a running turn's latest update is not cut. No Show more or Show all appears.
  • On: everything is cut as before, and the Prompt lines and Reply lines steppers show under it. Reset to defaults turns it off.
  • The mod's draw time on a 2,000-turn session is unchanged, also with every reply about 4,000 characters long: about 4 ms with nothing changed and 11 to 13 ms for a rebuild, against Herdr's 50 ms limit. Herdr's own drawing of long replies while scrolling cannot be timed on the test window, which has no wheel.
  • No notice or hint appears anywhere.

Not in this change

  • Code and output boxes that scroll sideways, and highlighting through fenced blocks, come next as their own change.

Left for the owner to check by hand

  • Scroll a long session with the wheel and say whether it feels as smooth as before.
