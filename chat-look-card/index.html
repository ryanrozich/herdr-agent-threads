A turn's numbers move to a card behind i

Real frames from Herdr 0.1.15 in an isolated test window on the mini. The session is synthetic. Both builds get the same presses on turn #7: select it, open it, click its "Worked for" line, then press i. The after build then gets ↑, i, a click on the info icon of #7's line, and Esc. Click any frame to open it full size. The arrow keys step through them and Esc closes.

The recordings

Frame by frame

What changed

  • The two stats lines and the legend leave the opened work.
  • i on the selected turn opens the Turn card over the bottom of the pane, built like the ? sheet. Context compacted comes first when it happened. Elapsed time stays on "Worked for" and is not repeated.
  • i, Esc or the close button closes it. While open it follows the selection. i no longer opens the turn or its work, and with nothing selected it does nothing.
  • An open "Worked for" line ends in a small info icon, dim on the selected turn and in the accent colour while the card shows that turn. A click on it opens or closes the card. A click elsewhere on the line opens or closes the line as before.
  • No setting, and the card's open state is not saved. A running turn's live line is unchanged.
  • The ? sheet row now reads "Show or hide the turn's numbers". The usage page and RECIPE say the same.
  • No notice or hint appears anywhere.

Left for the owner to check

  • The icon on a turn that is not selected shows only while the pointer is on its line. The test window has no pointer, so that half needs a check by hand: point at an open "Worked for" line of a turn that is not selected, and the icon should appear at its right end.
