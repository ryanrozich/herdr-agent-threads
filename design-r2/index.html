Agent Threads, as shipped

The Pen file now draws Agent Threads as it runs today, at herdr-agent-threads main a67b7dd (PRs 8 to 18): the chat look, the agent's state in the header, the new-reply dot, the one-row footer, the ? key sheet, pin numbers, and the Pinned column with its day pills. Frames

Click any frame to open it full size. Arrow keys step through the frames, Z switches between fit and actual size, and Esc closes. The last four images are the shipped mod, for comparison.

What changed since revision 1

  • Chat look (PR 8). Your message is a slate bubble on the right under its number and time, and the reply is plain text under the agent's name, with stats in one mono line. Day bands mark each day.
  • Pins (PRs 6, 8, 16, 18). p pins a turn and 1 to 5 return to it. Pins show in a Pinned column beside the list (or in the same pane under 480 px), with the pin number on each pinned turn's meta line. A compact day pill sits above a pin whose day differs from the pin above it.
  • Header (PR 16). A dot and word beside the badge say Working, Needs you, Done or Exited. Under 440 px the dot moves onto the badge. The jump-to-latest button gets a dot when a turn arrives below.
  • Footer and keys (PR 16). The footer is one row ending in ? keys, and ? opens a sheet with every key in four groups.
  • Removed from the design. Cost chips, the row layout, the three-harness frame and the remote states. The chat-look proposal's other pin layouts and the quote tracking frames were not built, so they are not in this file.

How it was checked

Each frame was exported and compared with the shipped mod's screenshots, which were taken in an isolated Herdr window on the same synthetic session. Colours are the running panel's, in the graphite and paper themes. The design does not show the live behaviour: scrolling, selection, the midnight change from Today to Yesterday, or how a theme other than graphite and paper looks.
