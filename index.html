Try it

  1. Install the branch:
    cd ~/herdr-agent-threads-try && git fetch && git checkout chat-look && git pull && ./install.sh
  2. Close the Threads pane and open it again with Cmd-Shift-T.
  3. Scroll the list. Each turn reads like a chat, with your message in a blue bubble on the right.
  4. Press Enter on a long turn to open it, and Esc to close it.
  5. Press p on two turns, then v. A Pinned pane opens beside Threads. Press v again to go back.
  6. Press , and pick other bubble colours. Reset to defaults puts back blue and purple.

A settings file that still holds the old defaults (2 and 3 lines) reads as the new ones, 4 and 6. Values you chose yourself stay.

What is built

  • The chat look. Your message is a bubble on the right, the reply is plain text across the pane, the number and time sit above, and time, tokens, model and tool calls sit below.
  • Collapse at 4 and 6 lines. Messages show 4 lines and replies 6 before "Show more". Settings allow 1 to 10 and 1 to 12.
  • Pinned view, option B. v opens a Pinned pane beside Threads from 600 px. Under 600 px the same pane switches to the pins, as option A. The spike on pane.split from a key press passed before the build.
  • Bubble colours. Blue for your messages and purple for agent messages by default, with white text at 5.2:1 and 5.7:1. Seven presets, plain for the theme's own grey, or any #rrggbb in the file.
  • Pin returns. This build includes the fix now on PR 6: 1 shows the turn from your prompt, or opens at the block you were reading under a "#8 starts above" line.

Notes for review

  • An open turn on a coloured bubble shows your message as plain text in white. Herdr draws Markdown in the theme's text colour whatever the bubble says, which is dark on blue in the light theme. Quotes keep their bar; link rules and code spans in your message show only with plain. The reply is unchanged.
  • Two departures from the design: the action bar stays as buttons, and the side padding is 16 px rather than 12, to line up with the rest of the pane.
  • v is now taken, so a quoteKey of v falls back to q with a note in settings.
  • One timing test (S1) failed once under load and passed on the rerun.
  • The README screenshots still show the old look. They can be retaken once you approve this look, since new images in the repo need your approval first.
  • Quote tracking is next, as its own PR.
