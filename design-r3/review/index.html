Agent Threads, as shipped

The Pen file now draws Agent Threads as it runs today, at herdr-agent-threads main 3d0b81c (PRs 8 to 23). Revision 3 adds pins in time order with one day pill per day (PR 19), compact Pinned rows (PR 22), a header with no pin button whose status goes to the terminal on a click (PR 23), and the outline (PR 21). Replies have no agent name over them, as herdr-agent-threads PR 24 ships. Revisions 1 and 2 and the Nov 8 chat-look proposal stay in git history.

Frames

Click any frame to open it full size. Arrow keys step through the frames, Z switches between fit and actual size, and Esc closes. The last five images are the shipped mod, for comparison.

What changed since revision 2

  • Pins in time order (PR 19). The Pinned column and the narrow Pinned view list pins oldest first, numbered 1 to 5 from the top, so each day shows once with its pill.
  • Compact Pinned rows (PR 22). A closed pin is one row: its digit, the turn number and time, two lines of the prompt and one of the reply, so five fit in the column.
  • Header (PR 23). The list's header has no pin button, so the session title gets the room. A click on the state goes to the terminal, as t does; under 440 px the badge takes the click. The Pinned view keeps its pin button, which unpins.
  • No name over a reply (PR 24). A reply starts straight under its message. The open turn's caption keeps what the reply leaves out, such as 6 tool calls (not shown). Agent messages keep their sender.
  • Outline (PR 21). o shows one row per turn under the same day bands, and the footer and the ? sheet list it. Enter and Space still open a turn.

How it was checked

Each frame was exported and compared with the shipped mod's screenshots, which were taken in an isolated Herdr window on the same synthetic session. Colours are the running panel's, in the graphite and paper themes. The design does not show the live behaviour: scrolling, selection, the midnight change from Today to Yesterday, or how a theme other than graphite and paper looks.
